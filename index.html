About

10+ years behind the mix.

14 Sticks Entertainments is a Lagos-based audio post and music production house founded by Mustapha Ishola — a sound designer, recording and mixing engineer, music producer and film composer.

Across more than a decade in film, television, music and studio environments, the work has earned an AMVCA for Best Sound and credits on a Top-5 Netflix title. From sound editorial to final delivery, the goal is the same: sound that serves the story.
